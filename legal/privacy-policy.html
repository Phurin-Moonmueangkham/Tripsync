TripSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TripSync ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the TripSync mobile and web application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Location information: your live location while using trip tracking and meeting point features.
  • Trip data: trip name, destination, route points, meeting point details, and group member statuses.
  • Device/app data: basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core features such as real-time trip coordination, member tracking, and SOS alerts.
  • Improve app reliability, performance, and safety-related experiences.
  • Support account access, troubleshooting, and fraud or abuse prevention.

3. Location Data

Location data is used to provide map, routing, group coordination, and meeting point functionality. You can stop sharing location by leaving an active trip or disabling permissions from your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared with service providers used to operate the app, including cloud and mapping services, only as needed to provide core functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations. Data may be deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • Review or update your profile details inside the app.
  • Control location permission through your device settings.
  • Request account/data assistance by contacting us.

8. Children

TripSync is not intended for children under the age required by local law without parental/guardian supervision.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
